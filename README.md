# Hearth & Harbor v1.5 — Little Households

Life-simulation update to the original mobile-first 2.5D storybook village.

- Six persistent residents each track food, energy, social connection, and fun.
- Resident moods reflect those needs and are visible in People, conversations, and the town.
- Residents temporarily visit the café, rest, or seek company when needs are critically low, then resume their schedules.
- Six individual three-step dreams, with one help opportunity per in-game day, consistent story outcomes, and a completion bonus.
- Town Stories records completed dreams; community meetups now lift resident needs.
- Existing saves are migrated and validated without changing the storage key.

All progress remains on-device. No server, account, or payment required. A full autonomous household simulator is not implemented yet; the resident needs and choices are an early simulation layer.
