# Hearth & Harbor — Quality-first roadmap (v2.2 through v3.0)

**Approved creative direction:** Cozy 2.5D storybook/pixel life simulator, originally 65% Sims-like household life / 35% Animal Crossing-like village life. Entirely original assets and writing. **iPhone-first, free single-player, GitHub Pages; no new paid infrastructure.**

## Core quality contract
- Prioritize **variety, depth, charm, and rewarding activities** over feature count.
- New activities must connect to existing needs, relationships, money, progress, seasons, or town stories.
- A system isn't considered finished until it has understandable UI, satisfying art/audio feedback, saves, accessibility options, balance checks, and device testing.
- All releases preserve older local saves. Export backups before any large migration.
- No rushed "complete" declarations for v2.5 or v3.0. Each is split into playtested milestones and expands only once the previous slice proves fun.
- At every release: test 320px and larger phone widths, landscape, touch gestures, restart/reload, economy exploits, NPC pathfinding, and main menus. Obtain physical iPhone Safari feedback before calling final polish complete.

## v2.2 — Around the Table (first playable slice built)
**12 recipes**, not 8. Every dish has its own ingredient list, presentation, and multi-step preparation flow.

First 12: Cinnamon Toast; Orchard Fruit Salad; Garden Vegetable Soup; Berry Pancakes; Sunny Morning Eggs; Herb Garden Noodles; Harbor Grilled Fish; Honeybun Berry Tart; Honey Cloud Biscuits; Orchard Apple Jam; Picnic Garden Skewers; Little Garden Dumplings.

15 preparation types/cues: slice, stir, flip, crack, knead, pour, season, bake, garnish, fill, stamp, simmer, thread, grill, steam. Some actions share a timing or touch foundation, but each recipe needs meaningfully different combinations and visual staging. Support accessible large-button alternatives, relaxed timing, low-stress quality scoring, varied art, and 12-recipe cookbook.

Pantry shopping and starter stock, cooking XP/mastery, meal storage/eating, old-save migration, favorite dishes for six neighbors, and meal sharing inside residential units. One relationship reward per neighbor per in-game day, with favorites rewarded more strongly. Future polish: deeper gesture-specific scoring, animation transitions, audio, recipe unlock discovery, leftovers/household table scenes, and real physical-device feedback.

**Exit:** Each recipe completed with touch and accessibility alternatives; save and reload preserved; fair prices; visible art differentiation; no duplicate serving rewards.

## v2.3 — A House Is a Home (first playable slice live; continuing development)
- **First slice implemented:** Guide residents inside their own residences, schedule five routines, choose among six room palettes, buy visible personal touches, and earn capped home comfort benefits. Remaining work: full household-wide control, richer building and deeper autonomy.
- **Controllable household switching:** household portraits and independent action queues for every family member, with clear pause/fast-forward and ownership indicators.
- **Deep decoration:** room layouts, walls and flooring, wallpaper, lighting, adjustable placement/rotation/snapping, colorways, custom furniture sets and a comfortable 2.5D dollhouse view. Prioritize touch ease with undo/redo, affordability preview, free placement grid options, sensible storage and collision protections.
- **Decoration matters:** comfort, style, mood, hobbies and home function improve when residents actually use well-furnished spaces. Functional kitchen upgrades make cooking convenient, bookshelves support reading, inviting seating supports conversations; avoid pay-to-win stat stacking or unavoidable chores.
- **Autonomous home routines:** bathing/hygiene, cooking, eating, socializing, relaxing, hobbies and bed use. Roommates cooperate or disagree based on needs and personality.
- **Meaningful goals:** household aspirations, family photo/memory board, errands, rent/upkeep decisions, personal projects, and style collections. Provide optional creative mode for decorating without budget limits.
- **Quality gates:** one gorgeous fully controllable household that remains fun during a 30-minute play session before rolling functionality to all residences.

## v2.4 — Hearts & Personalities
Creative license: make character-driven stories emotional but cozy, not punitive.
- Trait system with goals, social preferences, meaningful likes/dislikes and evolving personal tastes.
- Relationship layers: friendships, family, romance where appropriate, trust, compatibility, tension and reconciliation. Clear consent/eligibility logic, age-appropriate character interactions, no manipulative timers.
- Dating, celebrations, engagements and weddings as optional arcs; no compulsory marriage path.
- Memories and story consequences influence schedules, dialogue, gift preferences, household compatibility, social invitations and town events; make outcomes explainable to players.
- **Quality gate:** repeated interactions produce believable variety, not constant random soap opera; no duplicate event farms, impossible relationships or broken household moves.

## v2.5 — The Living Village (major expansion; multiple milestones)
Do not treat this as one fast patch. Build in slices with clear playtests:
1. **Village art foundation:** coherent tiles, lighting, seasonal palettes, unique places, readable navigation, rich pixel animation and environmental storytelling.
2. **Discovery and collecting:** fishing rework, bug collecting, fossils, museum displays with real donated items; seasonal and rarity logic, completion without unreasonable grind.
3. **Landscape and ecology:** flower hybrids, vegetable gardens that feed cooking, orchards, weather, animal visitors, public green spaces and surprising places to explore.
4. **Shops & local economy:** reliable shops, NPC shopkeepers, market orders, craft/cooking orders, shops that visually change as the village develops.
5. **Events and festivals:** festivals that are fully interactive, have bespoke games, dialogue, decoration, recipes and meaningful rewards; seasonal calendar robust across real-life play schedules.
6. **Town stewardship:** streets, parks, bridges, plazas and facilities upgraded through projects with visible and mechanical payoff; player agency without monotonous resource grinding.

**Exit:** Distinct districts worth exploring, seasonal play variety, accessible travel and saves, no unfinished placeholder collectibles or empty buildings. Extensive iPhone performance budgeting and bug fixes between slices.

## v3.0 — Generations (largest milestone; several major releases)
Another deliberate phased effort, only after households are deep and fun:
1. **Family relationships & life stages:** clear household membership and lineage data, toddlers/children/teens/adults/elders as suitable simulation progression, optional aging/pause, deeply supported child-friendly interactions.
2. **Care & routines:** parenting, mentorship, schooling/hobbies, intergenerational cooking, bedtime stories, celebrations; schedules that work without overwhelming micro-management.
3. **Inheritance & legacy:** family trees, keepsakes, household memories, property transfer, heirlooms, individual aspirations and life milestones, robust save migration and reversible choices.
4. **Generational village:** residents change careers, open shops, grow up, move homes, and maintain relationships through time; coherent timeline, town history and visual evolution.
5. **Balancing & accessibility:** opt-in aging, low-stress choices, creative mode, quality-of-life controls, reachable goals for short sessions, careful performance/memory management.

**Exit:** A full multigeneration household can be played through with continuity, meaningful growth, and no save corruption. Extensive regression and user playtests required. Do not fake depth using purely scripted checkboxes.

## Release cadence
Work in shippable increments. Prioritize v2.2 cooking variety, then v2.3 first fully controllable home. In parallel keep a dedicated **visual art polish and QA track**. Do not ship giant v2.5/v3.0 features until their smaller slices meet the quality bar.
